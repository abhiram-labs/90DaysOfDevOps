# My 90-Day DevOps & Cloud Learning Plan

## 1. My Current Level
I have a basic understanding of DevOps concepts and familiarity with some tools in the DevOps ecosystem. I want to strengthen my fundamentals, improve my practical skills, and develop a structured understanding of how these tools work together in real-world workflows.

## 2. My Understanding of DevOps & Cloud
DevOps brings development and operations together through collaboration, automation, CI/CD, monitoring, and reliable software delivery. Cloud engineering focuses on building, deploying, and managing scalable infrastructure and services using cloud platforms such as AWS. Both areas play an important role in delivering applications efficiently and reliably.

## 3. Why I Am Taking This Challenge
I want to build on my existing knowledge, gain more hands-on experience, and develop the practical skills required for real-world DevOps and Cloud Engineering tasks. This 90-day challenge will help me follow a structured learning path, practise consistently, and build projects that demonstrate my skills.

## 4. My Three Goals for 90 Days
1. Strengthen my knowledge of Linux, networking, Git, GitHub, and Bash scripting.
2. Gain practical experience with Docker, CI/CD pipelines, AWS fundamentals, and introductory Kubernetes.
3. Complete at least two hands-on projects, document my work on GitHub, and prepare for DevOps and Cloud Engineering opportunities.

## 5. My Three Core Skills
- Linux administration, command-line usage, and troubleshooting
- Containerization with Docker and CI/CD automation
- Cloud infrastructure fundamentals and application deployment

## 6. My Consistency Commitment
I will aim to complete a practical task every day, document what I learn, and push my work to GitHub when appropriate. On busy days, I will dedicate at least 20 minutes to focused practice rather than skip completely. Every Sunday, I will review my progress and adjust my priorities for the following week.

**My commitment:** Over the next 90 days, I will focus on consistency, hands-on learning, and measurable progress. My goal is to build strong fundamentals, gain practical experience, and become better prepared for real-world DevOps and Cloud Engineering challenges.
