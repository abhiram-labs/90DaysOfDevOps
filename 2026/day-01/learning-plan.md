# My 90-Day DevOps Learning Plan

## My Understanding of DevOps and Cloud
DevOps helps us build, test, and deploy applications using tools and automation. Cloud Computing allows us to run applications and manage servers using services like AWS.

## Why I Am Starting
I want to learn DevOps and Cloud in a practical way. I want to understand how the tools work, practise them, and build projects on my own.

## My 3 Goals
1. Learn and practise Linux, Git, networking, and scripting.
2. Get hands-on experience with Docker, CI/CD, AWS, Terraform, and Kubernetes.
3. Build projects, upload my work to GitHub, and prepare for DevOps interviews.

## My 3 Main Skills
- Linux commands and troubleshooting.
- Docker and CI/CD pipelines.
- AWS and cloud infrastructure.

## My Learning Schedule
I will spend around 3–5 hours every day learning and practising. I plan to follow a fast-track approach and cover multiple challenge days when I understand the topics and complete the practical tasks.

## How I Will Stay Consistent
- Practise every day instead of only watching videos.
- Make notes and upload my work to GitHub.
- Revise topics that I find difficult.
- Track my progress and complete tasks one by one.

## My Commitment
I will stay consistent, learn by doing, and improve my skills every day. My goal is to gain practical knowledge and become confident in DevOps and Cloud.
